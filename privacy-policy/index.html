Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: March 22, 2026

Introduction

Baton Rouge Siding Installation ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website redsticksidingandroof.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our Site or submit information through our contact forms.

By using our Site, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the Site.

Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide

When you fill out a contact form or request an estimate on our Site, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Service interest or project details
  • Any additional information you choose to provide in the message field

This information is submitted voluntarily through our contact forms powered by Web3Forms, a third-party form processing service. Web3Forms processes your submission and delivers it to us via email. Web3Forms does not store your personal data beyond the time needed to deliver the message.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Site, certain information is collected automatically through the following tools:

  • Google Analytics (GA4): We use Google Analytics to collect anonymized data about how visitors interact with our Site, including pages visited, time spent on pages, referral sources, device type, browser type, and approximate geographic location. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this data. You can learn more about Google's data practices at Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Microsoft Clarity: We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how users interact with our Site through session recordings and heatmaps. Clarity may collect data such as mouse movements, clicks, scroll behavior, and page interactions. This data is anonymized and does not include personal information. You can learn more at Microsoft's Privacy Statement.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide requested estimates
  • To contact you about our siding services
  • To improve our website and user experience
  • To analyze site traffic and usage patterns
  • To maintain and optimize our marketing efforts

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We share form submission data with Web3Forms for form processing, and analytics data is processed by Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity as described above.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal requests by public authorities.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our Site uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and collect analytics data. Cookies are small data files stored on your device. The cookies on our Site are primarily used by:

  • Google Analytics — for website traffic analysis
  • Microsoft Clarity — for user behavior analysis and session replay

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of the Site.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Links

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of analytics tracking by using browser settings or opt-out tools provided by Google and Microsoft

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
