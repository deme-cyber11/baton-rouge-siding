Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about siding installation, materials, costs, and timelines in Baton Rouge.

How much does siding installation cost in Baton Rouge?
Siding installation in Baton Rouge typically ranges from $6,000 to $20,000 for a full home. Vinyl siding averages $7–$12 per sq ft installed. Fiber cement (HardiePlank) runs $10–$18 per sq ft. We provide free in-home estimates.
What siding material is best for Louisiana's climate?
Fiber cement siding (HardiePlank) is our top recommendation for Baton Rouge — it resists humidity, termites, and hurricane-force winds. High-quality vinyl is a cost-effective alternative. Avoid wood siding in Louisiana's wet climate.
Do you offer storm damage siding repair?
Yes — we handle insurance claims for storm and hail damage. Louisiana homeowners policies often cover storm-damaged siding. We document damage, work with adjusters, and handle all repairs.
Do you serve Gonzales, Prairieville, and Zachary?
Yes — we serve all of East Baton Rouge Parish and surrounding areas including Gonzales, Prairieville, Zachary, Baker, Port Allen, Walker, and Denham Springs.
How long does a full siding replacement take?
Most full home siding replacements take 3–7 days depending on home size and siding material. We handle all tear-off, installation, trim, and cleanup.

Can you match new siding to my existing siding for a repair?

In many cases, yes. For vinyl siding, manufacturers offer wide color and profile libraries, and we can often find a close match. For fiber cement, James Hardie's ColorPlus technology provides consistent color matches. However, if siding has faded over the years, a perfect match may require replacing an entire wall section. We'll assess the situation honestly during your free estimate.

Do I need to be home during the siding installation?

You don't need to be home every minute, but we ask that someone be available on the first day to walk through expectations and confirm any details. Our crews work on the exterior of your home, so you can go about your normal routine. We'll notify you when the job is complete for a final walkthrough.

Is a permit required for siding installation in Baton Rouge?

Permit requirements vary by parish and project scope. In many cases, like-for-like siding replacement does not require a permit, but adding new siding to a previously unclad surface may. We handle all permitting requirements and ensure the work is fully code-compliant in East Baton Rouge Parish and surrounding areas.

What warranty comes with new siding?

Warranties vary by material. James Hardie fiber cement carries a 30-year limited warranty. Most vinyl products offer lifetime manufacturer warranties. We also provide our own workmanship warranty on every installation. Proper installation by a certified contractor is required to keep manufacturer warranties valid — which is exactly why our crews follow all installation specifications.
