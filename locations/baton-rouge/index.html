Baton Rouge's Trusted Siding Installation Experts

As Louisiana's capital city and the heart of East Baton Rouge Parish, Baton Rouge is home to a diverse range of architectural styles and neighborhoods. From the stately homes in the Garden District to the mid-century ranches of Broadmoor and the growing subdivisions of Sherwood Forest, every Baton Rouge home deserves siding that protects and beautifies. Our licensed and insured team specializes in siding installation, repair, and replacement for homeowners throughout the greater Baton Rouge area.

Baton Rouge's subtropical climate brings intense summer heat, heavy rainfall, high humidity, and the constant threat of hurricane season. These conditions take a toll on exterior cladding, making quality siding materials and expert installation essential. We work with vinyl, fiber cement (HardiePlank), and wood siding — recommending the best option for your home's style, budget, and the unique demands of the Louisiana climate. Whether you live near the LSU campus, in the bustling Mid City neighborhood, or in the established communities along Highland Road, we deliver craftsmanship that lasts.

Our Baton Rouge siding services cover everything from full home re-siding to targeted storm damage repair. We handle insurance claims for hurricane and hail damage, provide free in-home estimates, and complete most projects in less than a week. Homeowners in downtown Baton Rouge, the neighborhoods surrounding the State Capitol, and the rapidly developing areas south of I-10 all rely on us for professional results at fair prices.

Beyond residential siding installation, we also serve commercial properties and multi-family buildings throughout East Baton Rouge Parish. From Coursey Boulevard to Airline Highway, from Jefferson Highway to Plank Road, our crews are familiar with every corner of the city. We take pride in being Baton Rouge's go-to siding contractor — delivering the same attention to detail whether we're installing siding on a starter home or a custom-built estate.
