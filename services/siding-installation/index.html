Professional Siding Installation for Baton Rouge Homes

Your home's siding is more than just a visual feature. It is the primary barrier between your family and Louisiana's intense heat, driving rain, and seasonal hurricanes. At Red Stick Siding & Roof, we provide full-service siding installation for new construction, major renovations, and complete re-siding projects across East Baton Rouge Parish and the surrounding communities. Our licensed and insured crews work with every major material, including vinyl, fiber cement (HardiePlank), natural wood, engineered wood, and aluminum, giving you a single trusted source for any exterior cladding project.

Every installation begins with a free on-site consultation where we assess your home's structure, discuss your goals, and walk you through the advantages and trade-offs of each siding material for your specific situation. We measure precisely, account for every window, door, soffit, and fascia detail, and deliver a comprehensive written estimate that covers materials, labor, trim, flashing, moisture barriers, and cleanup. There are no surprises and no change orders unless you request additional work. From pulling permits to passing the final inspection, we manage the entire process so you can focus on choosing the color and style you love.

What separates a great siding installation from a mediocre one is the work nobody sees: the house wrap lapped and taped correctly, the flashing integrated around every window and penetration, the nailing patterns that allow panels to expand and contract without buckling, and the ventilation gaps that prevent moisture from being trapped against the sheathing. Our crews are trained to follow manufacturer installation guidelines to the letter, which protects your warranty and ensures your siding performs the way it was engineered to in Baton Rouge's challenging climate. The result is a weathertight, beautiful exterior that adds lasting value to your home.

What to Expect During Your Siding Installation

Every siding installation we perform follows a consistent process designed to minimize disruption and deliver lasting results. After your free estimate is accepted, we schedule the job at a time that works for you and order your materials in advance so there are no mid-project delays.

On installation day, our crew arrives on time and sets up protective tarps and staging to keep your landscaping and walkways safe. Existing siding is carefully removed, and the underlying sheathing is inspected for any moisture damage that needs to be addressed. A water-resistive barrier is installed per building code before the new siding goes on.

Installation is carried out following manufacturer specifications for the material you've chosen. Our crews take care with every fastener, seam, and trim piece. When the job is done, we clean up fully — taking all old materials off your property — and walk you through the finished work before we leave.
