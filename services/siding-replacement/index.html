When You Need Full Siding Replacement

Siding replacement is the right call when the problem isn't one section — it's the whole system. Most Louisiana homes need full replacement when siding has reached the end of its useful life (typically 20–30 years for vinyl, longer for fiber cement), when damage from storms or moisture intrusion is widespread, or when multiple problem areas have developed across different elevations of the home.

The thing about aging siding in Louisiana is that the problems compound quickly. Siding that was installed 25 years ago wasn't designed for today's understanding of proper moisture management. The housewrap underneath — if there is one — may have failed. The flashing around windows may be corroded or poorly sealed. You're not just replacing the cladding; you're getting a second chance to do the entire moisture management system correctly.

If you're seeing issues on multiple walls, getting quotes that involve extensive repairs across different areas, or preparing a home for sale, replacement typically offers better long-term value than a patchwork of repairs. We'll tell you honestly which direction makes sense after we see the home.

How Siding Replacement Works

Full replacement is a systematic process. Every step matters — especially the ones you can't see once the new siding is on.

1
Free On-Site Assessment — We inspect the full exterior, probe problem areas, and look for signs of hidden moisture damage. You get a clear picture of what's there before any quote is written.
2
Written, Itemized Quote — Tear-off, disposal, housewrap, flashing, panels, trim, labor, and timeline — all priced and itemized before we start. If we find damaged sheathing once the old siding comes off, we stop and show you before touching anything additional.
3
Full Tear-Off and Substrate Inspection — Everything comes off. Once the old cladding is removed, we inspect every inch of sheathing for rot, moisture damage, and soft spots. This is when hidden problems surface — and why we always include a clear policy on how we handle them.
4
New Moisture Barrier Installation — Fresh water-resistive barrier, lapped correctly, taped at seams. Flashing re-done at every window, door, and penetration. This is where most replacement jobs differentiate themselves — or don't.
5
Panel Installation, Trim, and Final Walkthrough — New siding installed per manufacturer specification. Trim, corners, soffits, and fascia integrated cleanly. We walk every seam with you before the invoice — if anything isn't right, we fix it first.

What Every Replacement Job Includes

  • Complete tear-off of existing siding and trim
  • Full substrate inspection — every wall, every elevation
  • New water-resistive barrier, properly lapped and taped
  • New flashing at all windows, doors, and penetrations
  • New siding panels per manufacturer specification
  • New trim, corners, and soffit integration
  • Disposal of all old materials — hauled off-site
  • Written fixed-price quote — no surprise change orders
  • Manufacturer and workmanship warranty

What Does Siding Replacement Cost?

Honest ranges for a complete re-siding job in the Baton Rouge market:

Vinyl siding: $5,000–$13,000 for an average home
Fiber cement (James Hardie): $9,000–$19,000
Wood / cedar siding: $12,000–$28,000
Old siding tear-off: $1,000–$3,000 (included in above ranges)

Cost is driven by home size, material choice, existing siding removal, and substrate condition. If hidden rot or moisture damage is found during tear-off, we quote additional substrate work separately before touching it. The estimate is free, in-person, and written.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a full siding replacement take?

Most average Baton Rouge homes take 4–8 days for a complete replacement. Larger homes, multi-story homes, or homes with significant substrate damage may take longer. We schedule materials to arrive before the crew does so there are no mid-job delays waiting on supplies.

Can I stay in my home during siding replacement?

Yes. Your home is accessible throughout the project. Exterior work doesn't affect normal indoor living. We keep staging and debris organized and your walkways clear at the end of each day. You'll be notified each morning before the crew arrives.

Do you handle permits for siding replacement?

Permit requirements for siding replacement vary by jurisdiction in the greater Baton Rouge area. We'll determine whether a permit is required for your specific project during the estimate and handle the application if needed. It's included in the job — you don't need to deal with the permitting office.

What if rotted sheathing is found under the old siding?

We stop work and show you photos before proceeding. Substrate repair is quoted separately at that moment with a clear price — you approve before anything additional is touched. We never unilaterally expand scope, and we never cover damaged substrate with new siding. That's how problems get buried instead of solved.

Our schedule fills 3–6 weeks out. If you have a sale deadline, HOA requirement, or storm season approaching, call now. The estimate is free and written.
