Professional Siding Replacement for Baton Rouge Homes

When patching and repairs are no longer enough, a full siding replacement gives your Baton Rouge home a fresh start. Years of exposure to Louisiana's punishing heat, humidity, and seasonal storms take a toll on any exterior cladding, and there comes a point when the smartest investment is to strip the old material down to the sheathing and start with brand-new panels. Our licensed and insured crews handle every phase of the project, from pulling permits with East Baton Rouge Parish to the final cleanup, so you have a single point of contact throughout the entire process.

A complete siding replacement is also your opportunity to upgrade. Many Baton Rouge homes built in the 1970s through 1990s still have original aluminum or low-grade vinyl siding that no longer provides adequate weather protection or energy efficiency. When we remove the old material, we inspect the underlying sheathing and house wrap for hidden moisture damage, rot, or mold, making structural repairs before any new siding goes up. This thorough approach ensures the new installation performs at its best and protects your home for decades to come, rather than simply covering up problems that will worsen over time.

We offer replacement in every major siding material, including vinyl, fiber cement (HardiePlank), engineered wood, and natural cedar. During your free on-site consultation, we walk you through the pros and cons of each option for your specific home, neighborhood, and budget, and we provide a detailed, written estimate that covers materials, labor, permits, old siding disposal, and any necessary structural repairs. There are no hidden fees and no surprises on installation day.

  • All permits pulled and inspections coordinated on your behalf
