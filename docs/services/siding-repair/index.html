Fast, reliable siding repair for storm damage, cracks, warping, and moisture issues on all siding types.

Professional Siding Repair for Baton Rouge Homes

Living in Baton Rouge means your siding takes a beating. Between summer thunderstorms that hurl debris at your walls, hailstones that crack and dent panels, and the relentless humidity that breeds mold and moisture intrusion, even well-installed siding eventually needs repair. Our licensed and insured repair crews respond quickly throughout the Capital Region, arriving with the tools, materials, and expertise to fix the problem before it spreads to the sheathing, insulation, or framing behind your walls.

We repair every type of residential siding found on Baton Rouge homes, including vinyl, fiber cement (HardiePlank), aluminum, wood, and engineered wood products. Whether you have a single cracked panel from a fallen branch, a section of warped boards caused by trapped moisture, or widespread wind damage from the latest tropical system, we diagnose the root cause, not just the visible symptom. That means we check for water infiltration behind the damaged area, inspect the house wrap and flashing, and address any underlying issues so the same problem does not return next season.

We also work directly with homeowners insurance companies to streamline storm damage claims. Our team documents the damage with detailed photos and measurements, provides your adjuster with a professional scope of work, and coordinates the repair timeline so you get your home restored as quickly as possible. If you have noticed loose panels, gaps at seams, bubbling paint on fiber cement, or soft spots in your wood siding, do not wait for the next storm to make it worse. Call us for a free inspection and repair estimate today.
