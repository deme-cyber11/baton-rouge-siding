Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Effective Date: March 1, 2026

Baton Rouge Siding Installation ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website batonrougesiding.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our Site or use our services. By accessing or using our Site, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide

When you fill out a contact form, request an estimate, or otherwise communicate with us, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Service address or project location
  • Description of your siding project or service needs
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide in form submissions

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Site, certain information is collected automatically through cookies and tracking technologies, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring website or URL
  • Pages viewed and time spent on each page
  • Date and time of your visit
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • Click patterns and scrolling behavior

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Respond to inquiries: To contact you regarding your estimate request, question, or service inquiry.
  • Provide services: To schedule consultations, prepare estimates, and coordinate siding installation or repair work.
  • Improve our website: To analyze how visitors use our Site so we can enhance the user experience and content.
  • Communicate with you: To send follow-up communications related to your inquiry or project.
  • Comply with legal obligations: To fulfill any legal or regulatory requirements applicable to our business.

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Form Processing

Contact form submissions on our Site are processed through Web3Forms. When you submit a form, your information (name, email, phone number, and message) is transmitted through Web3Forms' servers and delivered to our team via email. Web3Forms does not store your submission data permanently. Their privacy policy is available at web3forms.com/privacy.

4. Analytics and Tracking

Our Site uses the following analytics services:

  • Google Analytics (GA4): We use Google Analytics 4 to analyze website traffic and user behavior. Google Analytics collects data through cookies and generates statistical reports about website activity. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Microsoft Clarity: We use Microsoft Clarity for behavioral analytics, including session recordings and heatmaps. Clarity collects anonymized interaction data to help us understand user engagement. Microsoft's privacy policy is available at privacy.microsoft.com.

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Disabling cookies may affect certain features on our Site.

5. Information Sharing

We may share your information in limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted third-party providers who assist in operating our website and business (form processing, analytics, hosting). These providers are obligated to use your information only to perform services on our behalf.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
  • Protection of rights: When necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction, including SSL/TLS encryption for data transmitted through our Site. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, typically for the duration of a business relationship or project, plus any additional period required by applicable law. Analytics data is retained in accordance with the default settings of each analytics platform.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Opt-out: Opt out of analytics tracking by adjusting your browser settings or using the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 18, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
