About Baton Rouge Siding Installation

Locally Owned. Licensed & Insured. Built for Louisiana Weather.

Your Trusted Baton Rouge Siding Experts

Baton Rouge Siding Installation is a locally owned and operated siding company proudly serving homeowners and businesses across East Baton Rouge Parish and the surrounding communities. We are fully licensed and insured, and our team brings over 15 years of hands-on experience installing, repairing, and replacing siding on residential and commercial properties throughout the greater Baton Rouge area. With more than 500 homes completed, we have earned a reputation for quality craftsmanship, honest pricing, and dependable service.

We understand the unique challenges that Louisiana's climate presents to your home's exterior. Baton Rouge's intense humidity, heavy rainfall, scorching summers, and the ever-present threat of hurricane-force winds demand siding materials and installation techniques built to endure. Our crews are factory-trained to work with all major siding materials — including vinyl, fiber cement (HardiePlank), wood, and LP SmartSide — and install them to manufacturer specifications so your siding stands up to whatever Mother Nature throws at it, season after season.

Whether you need a full siding replacement after storm damage, a targeted repair to restore your home's curb appeal, or a brand-new installation on new construction, we handle it all. We also specialize in storm damage repair and insurance claim assistance, helping Baton Rouge homeowners navigate the claims process from start to finish. Every project begins with a free, no-obligation in-home estimate and ends with a thorough cleanup and final walkthrough. Our goal is simple: protect your home, exceed your expectations, and earn your recommendation to friends and neighbors.
