Siding Installation Questions

Everything Baton Rouge homeowners need to know about siding installation, repair, and replacement.

How much does siding installation cost in Baton Rouge?
Siding installation in Baton Rouge typically ranges from $6,000 to $20,000 for a full home. Vinyl siding averages $7–$12 per sq ft installed, making it the most budget-friendly option. Fiber cement (HardiePlank) runs $10–$18 per sq ft but offers superior durability for Louisiana's climate. Final pricing depends on your home's size, the siding material you choose, and the complexity of the installation. We provide free in-home estimates so you know the exact cost before any work begins.
What siding material is best for Louisiana's climate?
Fiber cement siding (HardiePlank) is our top recommendation for Baton Rouge and the surrounding area. It is engineered to resist humidity, termites, and hurricane-force winds — the three biggest threats to your home's exterior in Louisiana. High-quality vinyl siding is a cost-effective alternative that also performs well in our climate. We generally advise against wood siding in Louisiana due to the intense moisture and termite pressure. Our team will help you choose the best material for your home and budget.
Do you offer storm damage siding repair?
Yes — storm damage siding repair is one of our specialties. We handle the entire insurance claims process for storm, hail, and wind damage. Our team documents all damage, works directly with your insurance adjuster, and completes all repairs once your claim is approved. Louisiana homeowners policies frequently cover storm-damaged siding, so many of our customers pay little to nothing out of pocket for covered repairs.
Do you serve Gonzales, Prairieville, and Zachary?
Yes — we proudly serve all of East Baton Rouge Parish and the surrounding communities. Our service area includes Baton Rouge, Gonzales, Prairieville, Zachary, Baker, Port Allen, Walker, Denham Springs, Sorrento, and Plaquemine. If you are located in the greater Baton Rouge area and need siding installation or repair, give us a call for a free estimate.
How long does a full siding replacement take?
Most full home siding replacements in Baton Rouge take 3–7 days depending on the size of your home, the siding material selected, and weather conditions. We handle all tear-off of existing siding, new installation, trim work, and complete cleanup. Our crews work efficiently to minimize disruption to your daily routine, and we communicate timelines clearly before the project begins.
