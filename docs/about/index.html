About Baton Rouge Siding Installation

Licensed and insured siding professionals proudly serving East Baton Rouge Parish and surrounding communities.

Our Story

Baton Rouge Siding Installation was founded with a simple mission: deliver the highest quality siding services to homeowners across East Baton Rouge Parish and the surrounding areas. We saw too many homeowners stuck with shoddy workmanship, overpriced estimates, and contractors who disappeared mid-project.

We built our company on the belief that every homeowner deserves a siding contractor they can trust — one who shows up on time, communicates clearly, uses quality materials, and stands behind every installation. Today, we're proud to be one of the most trusted siding companies in Baton Rouge.

From vinyl and fiber cement to wood siding and full home replacements, our team handles every project with the same attention to detail and commitment to excellence. We treat your home like it's our own.

Our Mission

To protect and beautify every home we touch with expert siding installation, honest pricing, and unmatched customer service.

Committed to Quality

Every siding project we take on is backed by our commitment to using only premium materials from trusted manufacturers. Whether it's James Hardie fiber cement, CertainTeed vinyl, or LP SmartSide engineered wood, we never cut corners on what goes on your home.

Our installation crews are factory-trained and follow manufacturer specifications to the letter. That means your siding warranty stays intact and your home stays protected for decades to come.
